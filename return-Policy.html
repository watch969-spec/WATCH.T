⚠ Important Notice Please contact our support team before sending any product back. Returns and refunds are subject to the eligibility conditions listed below. Products sent without prior approval may not be accepted.

1. Return Eligibility

If a return or replacement is permitted for your order, please contact us within 7 days of delivery (or as communicated on the product page at the time of purchase).

  • The watch must be unused and in its original condition.
  • Original packaging, tags, warranty card and included accessories must be retained.
  • Proof of purchase or your order number is required.
  • Products damaged through misuse, accidents or normal wear may not qualify.
  • Items must be returned with all free gifts or bundled items, if any.

2. Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned or refunded:

  • Products marked as "Final Sale" or "Non-Returnable" on the product page.
  • Personal care items (for hygiene reasons).
  • Products that have been used, washed, altered or damaged by the customer.
  • Items returned without original packaging or missing accessories.
  • Gift cards and downloadable products.

3. Damaged, Defective or Incorrect Products

If you receive a damaged, defective or incorrect product, contact us within 48 hours of delivery. Please share your order number and clear photographs or a short video showing the issue. Our team will review the request and explain the available resolution.

For damaged or wrong items, we cover return shipping and offer a full replacement or refund as per your preference.

4. Replacement Policy

A replacement may be offered for an eligible damaged, defective or incorrect product, subject to verification and stock availability. If a replacement is unavailable, we will offer a full refund or an alternative product of equal value.

5. Refund Policy

Refunds are processed only after an eligible return has been received, inspected and approved.

  • Prepaid orders — Refunded to the original payment method within 5–7 business days after approval.
  • COD orders — Refunded via bank transfer or UPI within 5–7 business days. You will need to share your bank details.
  • Shipping charges (if any) are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error.
  • Bank or payment-provider processing may take an additional 2–3 days.

6. Order Cancellation

You can cancel your order before it is dispatched. Once shipped, the order cannot be cancelled — but you may still be eligible for a return after delivery, as per the eligibility rules above.

To cancel, contact us as soon as possible with your order number. Eligible refunds will be processed per our refund policy.

7. Return Shipping

Contact our support team before shipping any item back. We will guide you on the return process, pickup or courier options, and any applicable charges.

  • For damaged, defective or incorrect items — return shipping is free (we arrange pickup).
  • For change of mind or non-defect returns — return shipping charges may apply and be deducted from the refund.
  • Please ensure the item is securely packed in its original packaging to avoid transit damage.

8. How to Request a Return

  1. Keep your order number ready.
  2. Contact WATCH.T support (email or WhatsApp) and explain the issue.
  3. Provide requested photographs or a short video of the product.
  4. Wait for return instructions and approval before sending the item.
  5. Once approved, ship the item or wait for our pickup partner.
  6. After inspection, your refund or replacement will be processed.

9. Policy Updates

WATCH.T may update this policy from time to time. The applicable terms and any rights provided by law will continue to apply to your purchase. Please review this page periodically to stay informed.
