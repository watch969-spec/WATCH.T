Shipping Policy

Last Updated: October 13, 2026

1. Order Processing

Orders are processed within 1–3 business days after confirmation. Orders placed on Sundays or public holidays may take additional time to process.

2. Shipping and Delivery Time

Estimated delivery time is 3–7 business days for most locations in India. Delivery to remote areas may take longer.

3. Shipping Charges

Shipping charges, if applicable, will be displayed at checkout before the order is placed. Free shipping may be available on selected products or orders.

4. Order Tracking

Once your order has been shipped, tracking details may be shared through SMS, email, or WhatsApp, where available.

5. Delayed or Lost Shipments

Delivery times are estimates and may be affected by weather conditions, courier delays, public holidays, or other circumstances beyond our control. Please contact our customer support team if your order is delayed.

6. Incorrect Shipping Address

Customers are responsible for providing a correct and complete shipping address and contact number. Please contact us as soon as possible if you need to correct your address after placing an order.

7. Damaged Packages

If your package arrives damaged, please contact our customer support team as soon as possible and provide photographs of the package and product.

8. Contact Us

For shipping-related questions, please contact us using the contact details provided on our website.

📦 Note: All delivery timelines are estimates and may vary based on your location, courier partner and order volume.
