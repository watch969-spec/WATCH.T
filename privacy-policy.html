⚠ Your Rights You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information at any time. Contact us to exercise these rights. We never sell your data to third parties.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use our website, we may collect:

  • Name and contact information (email, phone number).
  • Shipping and billing address.
  • Order and purchase details (products, quantities, amount).
  • Information you provide when contacting customer support.
  • Basic technical information such as browser type, device type, IP address, and approximate location (where collected).
  • Cookies and usage data (see section 3).

What we do NOT collect: We do not store your full payment card numbers, CVV, or UPI PIN. Payment details are handled securely by our payment provider (e.g., Razorpay) or collected at the time of COD delivery.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Process, pack, and deliver your orders.
  • Send order confirmations, invoices, and shipping updates.
  • Respond to your questions and support requests.
  • Improve our website, products, and customer experience.
  • Prevent fraud and protect our website and users.
  • Send promotional offers, updates, or newsletters (only if you opt in — you can unsubscribe anytime).
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to support functionality, remember preferences, and understand usage. Here's how we use them:

  • Essential cookies: Required for the website to work (cart, login, checkout). These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand how visitors use the site (e.g., Google Analytics). You can opt out.
  • Marketing cookies: Used to show relevant ads (e.g., Meta Pixel). You can opt out.

You can manage or disable cookies in your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies may break parts of the website (like the cart).

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share necessary information only with trusted service providers who help us operate our business:

  • Delivery/courier partners (name, address, phone for delivery).
  • Payment processors (to process payments securely).
  • Website hosting and analytics providers.
  • Customer support services.
  • Legal authorities — only when required by applicable law.

5. Payment Security

All online payments are processed through secure third-party payment gateways (such as Razorpay). These gateways use industry-standard encryption (SSL/TLS). We do not store your full card details, CVV, or UPI PIN on our servers.

Please review the privacy policy of your chosen payment provider for more details on how they protect your information.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, misuse, loss, or disclosure:

  • SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmission.
  • Restricted access to customer data (only authorized staff).
  • Regular security reviews of our systems.

However, no method of online transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be 100% secure. Please keep your account credentials safe and contact us immediately if you suspect any unauthorized activity.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy:

  • Order records: Retained for 3 years (for warranty, returns, and legal compliance).
  • Account information: Retained until you request deletion.
  • Marketing data: Until you unsubscribe.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law (including India's DPDP Act, 2023), you have the right to:

  • Access your personal information we hold.
  • Correct any inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Delete your personal information (subject to legal retention).
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications.
  • Opt out of cookies via your browser settings.
  • Raise a grievance with our Grievance Officer.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in the "Contact Us" section below. We aim to respond within 30 days.

9. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites (e.g., payment gateways, social media). We are not responsible for their privacy practices or content. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies before sharing personal information.

10. Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page along with the revised update date at the top. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Your continued use of the website after changes means you accept the updated policy.
